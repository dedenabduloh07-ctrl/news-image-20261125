A newspaper front page summing up the world news of **Wednesday 25 November 2026**, drawn for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125).

## Stories the image covers

- **Lead — Strait of Hormuz:** two oil tankers hit by naval mines, ablaze; Brent crude jumps.
- **Houthis strike Saudi Arabia:** Ras Tanura refinery attacked; 11 hurt in a Riyadh skyscraper strike the day before.
- **Huawei found guilty** in a US court of conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud.
- **China–Pakistan in space:** Shenzhou-24 docks carrying Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, the first foreign astronaut on China's station.
- **US tariffs rise:** new tariffs proposed on Brazil imports and 14 trading partners; Q3 GDP up 4.3%; Wall Street slides.
- **Macy's Thanksgiving Parade postponed** — the first postponement in 100 years — as a powerful storm threatens the US East Coast.
- **World in brief:** Kraków shooting (at least six killed), Cartagena hostage siege (25 hostages), Sudan drone strikes (19 killed), COP31 climate talks fail.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` when that variable is set). The image is drawn by `build_image.py` (Pillow) and committed to this repository; `main.py` only copies it, byte for byte, using the Python standard library.
